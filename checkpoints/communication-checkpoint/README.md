# Communication Checkpoint

For this checkpoint I recorded two short videos of myself (about 1 minute 30 each) with Loom. In both videos I say the same things: my name, my academic journey and my professional experience. The difference is how I say it. In the first one I act shy, nervous and stressed, and in the second one I am confident, relaxed and calm.

## Video 1: shy, nervous and stressed

https://www.loom.com/share/0ae6178ad43e447f937b59ec73979ed8

Here I speak quietly and fast, I don't look at the camera much, I hesitate and say "um" a lot, and my posture is closed. Even though the information is the same, it's harder to follow and I don't come across as sure of myself.

## Video 2: confident, relaxed and calm

https://www.loom.com/share/cd56959537ee418f80ce017fc9b0173c

Here I look at the camera, speak slower and clearer, take small pauses instead of filling them, and sit up straight. It's the same introduction, but it sounds much more convincing.

## What I learned

Doing both versions back to back showed me how much body language, voice and pace change the message. The words were almost the same, but in the second video I sound like someone you would want to hire. The things that made the biggest difference for me were slowing down, looking at the camera, and being okay with a short silence.
